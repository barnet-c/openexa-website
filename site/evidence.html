03Why agents winWhere the 90% comes from

Two components eliminated. Two compressed. The operator changed.

Cost of carry and counterparty risk carry more than 80% of the weight in a human-run operation. Agents don't hold inventory and don't wait on a counterparty, so both go to zero. Technology and management — the remaining fifth — are cut by 67% and 50%.
